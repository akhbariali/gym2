# A small launch for natural bodybuilding in Iran

This is a business hypothesis to test, not a market-size estimate or a guarantee of profitability. The site is still a demo shop; do not treat its sample prices as commercial forecasts.

## Position and first audience

Start with Persian-speaking beginner lifters who want sustainable muscle gain, have limited food/supplement budgets, and need a routine they can continue around work or study. Speak to a specific problem before widening into general sports and health.

Promise useful training, ordinary food, clear explanations and honest supplement decisions. Avoid claiming that a physique proves someone is drug-free. Do not sell “natural” as proof of safety or invent transformations, credentials, customers or testimonials.

## First 30 days

1. Interview 10–15 relevant lifters and a few local coaches. Ask what they tried, what they found confusing, what they bought and why they returned. Record their words and recurring problems.
2. Prepare seven reviewed Telegram posts before inviting readers. Publish one useful Telegram post per day and about three Instagram clips/carousels per week using the same researched topics. This is a proposed editorial schedule; the site's existing 48-hour article publisher has not been changed.
3. Use topics with an immediate action: “What to do after missing a workout,” “How to compare protein foods using today's prices,” “A simple training log,” and “When whey is convenient and when food is enough.” Include sources and corrections. Have an appropriately qualified reviewer check injury, disease and higher-risk supplement advice.
4. Film demonstrations with a local coach where possible, with permission. Begin with small gym collaborations and genuine reader questions rather than buying followers or scraping unsolicited messages.
5. Invite readers to the starter guide or calculator, then invite them to follow your real channel. Do not turn every post into a sale. Connect actual Telegram/Instagram URLs when available; the site currently has no invented social accounts.

The website path is: useful content → beginner guide/calculator → return through your channel → compare a supplement only when relevant. Health calculations stay in the browser. Avoid collecting sensitive health details through comments or public groups.

## What would count as evidence

Track relevant visitors, weekly returning readers, meaningful questions, forwarded/saved posts, channel joins, and genuine product enquiries. For every enquiry, learn why someone wanted the product and what stopped the purchase. Use consistent time windows. Views and follower counts alone do not establish willingness to pay.

Before buying significant stock, test whether readers return and whether qualified enquiries can produce a positive contribution margin:

`sale price − product cost − payment fees − packaging − delivery subsidy − expected returns/losses − acquisition cost`

Recalculate with actual supplier quotes and delivery conditions. Consider stock expiry, cash tied up in inventory and repricing when costs change. These are planning checks, not claims that a particular price or margin is achievable in Iran.

## Sales and product trust

Confirm the currently permitted retail/online sales model with Iran's Food and Drug Administration and the appropriate local licensing authority before enabling real supplement checkout. Ordinary website registration or a payment gateway should not be assumed to grant permission for supplement sales. This session did not verify the current legal requirements: the FDA website could not be retrieved reliably.

The official [TTAC site](https://www.ttac.ir/) was accessible and identifies itself as the Iran Food and Drug Administration's traceability system. Use its official authenticity-verification guidance for applicable products. An authenticity check does not replace seller authorization or guarantee suitability for an individual. Source from permitted suppliers and show real product/batch details rather than unsupported “original” claims.

The current homepage uses a licensed Mixkit training clip. Replace it with original, permissioned footage over time to make the brand more local and distinctive. No model endorsement or natural-status claim is made.

## Launch decision

This is a reasonable low-inventory experiment if you can publish useful Persian material consistently and establish a compliant, trustworthy supply route. Delay broader inventory spending until repeat use and real customer enquiries support it. The next step is audience interviews and a month of useful content, not a bigger app.
